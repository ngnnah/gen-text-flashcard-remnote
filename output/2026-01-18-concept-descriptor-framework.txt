# Concept Descriptor Framework

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hPNvPiYralc
Tags: #notetaking #remnote #learning

Concept Descriptor Framework :: A 5-step note-taking method that structures notes around concepts and descriptors, enabling automatic flashcard generation
  core principle ;; learning process matters more than time spent

Step 1: Identify Concepts :: Write down names of important things (key terms, nouns) from the material

Step 2: Define Concepts :: Write a concise 1-2 sentence definition capturing the core idea
  key question to ask ;; "What is the most important part of this idea?"

Step 3: Add Descriptors :: Add extra questions or details about each concept
  what to include ;; questions from material or your own curiosity

Descriptor :: A question or property indented underneath a concept
  formatting tip ;; use shorthand ("consistency" not "what is the consistency")

Step 4: Organize Hierarchically :: Indent descriptors under concepts using Tab to create nested structure
  benefit ;; breaks complex ideas into brain-sized components

Step 5: Use the Knowledge :: Apply notes through search, flashcard practice, and building upon them

Three benefits of this method >> structures ideas in your brain, creates searchable references, generates flashcards automatically
